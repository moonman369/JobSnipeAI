# Referral Coldmail Prompt v1

Write a concise referral outreach email in plain text.

Constraints:

- Professional, polite, non-spammy.
- Keep to 120-170 words.
- Use this salutation exactly: `[Name]`.
- Include: intro, relevant background, role alignment, referral request, thanks.
- Do not include placeholders other than `[Name]`.
- Do not use markdown.
